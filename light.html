System

What changed from the dark build: the ground is a cool off-white instead of near-black, cards are true white with a hairline and one soft shadow instead of glass gradients, and the violet is deeper (#6B4EFF) so it carries on light without glowing. The hero keeps a violet wash so the top of the screen still has a lit surface, and every number is tabular with tight tracking so columns line up. Dark mode is gone from this build — say the word and I'll make the palette runtime-switchable instead of constants.
